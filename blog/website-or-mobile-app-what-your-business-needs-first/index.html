Website or Mobile App: What Does Your Business Need First?

Almost every startup asks us this question. The honest answer depends on where your customers are and what stage your business is at.

Start with a website if…

People find you through Google or social links, you sell or take orders online, or you need to look credible fast. A website is quicker and cheaper to launch, works on every device, and — built SEO-ready — keeps bringing customers from search.

Start with an app if…

Customers use your service daily, you need push notifications, offline access or phone features like camera and location, or you are building a product people log into every day.

The path most of our clients take

Launch a website first, validate the idea, then add the app with the same backend. Message us your idea on WhatsApp and we will tell you honestly which one fits your stage.
