TikTok Account Price in Pakistan: What You Actually Pay in 2026

"TikTok account kitne ka milta hai?" is the first question almost every buyer in Pakistan asks — and the honest answer is: it depends on the account. A fresh account and a monetized UK account with earning already started are completely different products. This guide gives you the real market ranges in PKR, what moves the price up or down, and how to check an account before you pay for it.

Typical price ranges in Pakistan (PKR)

  • Fresh / new account (no followers): roughly Rs 1,200 – 2,500 — a clean, ready-to-use account you grow yourself
  • UK account, monetization-ready: roughly Rs 2,500 – 5,000 — set up for the UK region, the most popular choice for Pakistani creators who want Creator Rewards eligibility
  • USA monetized account: roughly Rs 8,000 and up — US region accounts in an earning program; price rises quickly with followers
  • Monetized account with ~10K followers: around Rs 20,000 – 25,000 in the current market
  • Earning already started / established accounts: Rs 30,000 to well over Rs 100,000 — priced on real followers, real views and proof of earnings

These are market ranges, not fixed shop prices. Every serious seller — including us — confirms the exact price for the exact account in chat, because two accounts with the same follower count can be worth very different amounts.

What changes the price

  1. Region — UK and USA accounts cost more than PK accounts because their views pay in pounds and dollars, and their monetization programs actually exist. Pakistan itself is not in TikTok's Creator Rewards program, which is exactly why foreign-region accounts are in demand here.
  2. Monetization status — "eligible", "approved" and "earning already started" are three different levels, and the price jumps at each one.
  3. Followers and views — real followers with real recent views are worth money; inflated numbers are worth nothing and usually get the account flagged.
  4. Account age and history — older accounts with a clean record (no strikes, no bans) are safer and pricier.
  5. LIVE and other features — LIVE access, an aged account and a clean username all add value.

Check these 5 things before you pay

  1. The account region (ask for proof in settings, not just a claim)
  2. Monetization status — see the actual monetization screen
  3. Original email access — you must receive the email the account was created with, and change the password and email yourself
  4. The account's standing — no active strikes or restrictions
  5. Recent views — real views on recent videos, not just a follower count

Common questions

Why is a UK/USA account better for a Pakistani creator?
Because TikTok pays creators in the UK/USA programs and does not pay for the same views in Pakistan. A UK or USA account lets a creator sitting in Pakistan work toward foreign-audience earnings.

Can I pay in PKR?
Yes — Pakistani sellers, including TikDevHub, take payment in PKR. The exact amount is confirmed on WhatsApp for the specific account you choose.

Is the cheapest account a good deal?
Usually not. Accounts sold far below market rate are often recovered by the original owner, banned, or fake-follower accounts. The checks above matter more than a small discount.
