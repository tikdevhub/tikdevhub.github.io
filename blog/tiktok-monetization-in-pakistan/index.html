The hard truth: Creator Rewards is NOT available in Pakistan

TikTok's Creator Rewards Program — the program that pays per qualified view — runs in only eight countries: the United States, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, Japan, South Korea, Brazil and Mexico. Pakistan is not on the list, and TikTok has announced no plan to add it.

  • An account based in Pakistan cannot join Creator Rewards
  • Views that come from inside Pakistan do not count as qualified views — they pay nothing, even on an eligible account
  • Using a VPN does not fix this; TikTok's own terms require you to actually be in an eligible country
  • To join, an eligible account needs 10,000+ followers and 100,000+ video views in the last 30 days

What DOES earn money from Pakistan

  1. TikTok LIVE gifts — viewers send coins, you receive diamonds, and diamonds can be cashed out. This works in Pakistan and is how many local creators earn their first real income.
  2. Brand deals and promotions — local businesses pay creators with an audience to promote products and services. No TikTok program needed.
  3. Selling your own services — video editing, account management, AI video creation, digital products. Your TikTok becomes the shop window; WhatsApp closes the sale.
  4. A UK or USA monetized account — the shortcut serious creators use, explained below.

The UK/USA account route — how it actually works

Because the paying programs live in the UK and USA, a creator sitting in Pakistan can buy an account that is already based in one of those countries and already approved for monetization. That clears the country lock. But be clear about the second half of the deal:

  • The account clears the country test — it does not clear the audience test
  • Your videos must win views from UK/USA viewers; views from Pakistan still pay nothing
  • That means content in English, posted for foreign trends and foreign timings
  • Creators who treat it like a real foreign channel earn; creators who post the same local content usually don't
