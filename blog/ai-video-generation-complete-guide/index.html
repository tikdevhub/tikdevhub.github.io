AI Video Generation: The Complete 2026 Guide

AI video in 2026 is a pipeline, not a single tool. Channels that publish daily combine script writing, AI voice, scene generation and automated editing into one repeatable system.

The pipeline, step by step

  1. Script — a hook in the first 3 seconds, then a clear story or lesson
  2. Voice — natural AI voiceover matched to the language and mood
  3. Scenes — generated visuals that follow the script, with consistent characters for series
  4. Edit & sound — pacing, captions, music and sound design
  5. Publish & repeat — the same system, every day

Short vs long-form

Shorts (30 seconds to 3 minutes) win discovery. Long-form (15 to 60+ minutes) wins watch time and loyal audiences — drama series and educational content work especially well. Most successful channels run both.
